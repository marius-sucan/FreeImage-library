Workspace W=$J/agents/jxl (bin/fijxl harness, bin/mkjxl crafts files with libjxl API, src/*.cpp). main has no JPEG XL: everything is a new bug in qpv (plugin commit 4b3d2f4, 2026-10-11; JXL-5 vendored libjxl c914936). ## JXL-1 Float JPEG XL images whose colours use the sRGB curve load with only a CICP tag, and FreeImage reads them as linear light
- Class: new-bug. Severity: Medium. Confidence: high.
- Where: PluginJXL.cpp:805-813 (AttachColor returns early `if(IsSRGB(&reader->data_enc)) return;` "sRGB is what an untagged bitmap means"); :794-796 (JXL_ROUTE_TO_SRGB sets CICP 1/13 only); applies when ChooseOutput picks FIT_FLOAT/RGBF/RGBAF (:663-673); 4b3d2f4.
- What: for float types FreeImage assumes linear sRGB (ColorManagement.cpp:527 DefaultProfile; AttachCICPProfile skips float at :2591 "floating point holds light"; GetCICPProfile(1,13) returns NULL). Lossless float files from cjxl/PFM input, lossy XYB files whose original encoding is sRGB, and the TO_SRGB route with float output come back sRGB-encoded with CICP 1/13 and no ICC. Only FreeImage_ConvertToLinear reads the tag correctly; ToneMapping (the AHK wrapper advises calling it directly for FITM_REQUIRED, freeimage-wrapper.ahk:1141), MakeThumbnail (Drago03 direct, Rescale.cpp:261), ConvertToICCProfile/ApplyICCProfile/SoftProof (source = linear-sRGB default), and EXR/HDR/PFM saves (tag lost) all treat the samples as linear.
- Evidence (src/demo_float.cpp; fs1.jxl lossy 64x48 gradient via `bin/mkjxl floatsrgb fs1.jxl 1`): loaded type=11 CICP=1/13/0/1 icc=0, MustTonemap 2; ConvertToLinear vs hand-decoded sRGB mean 0 max 0; ToneMapping tmo=0 direct vs truth mean 38.25 max 85 (via ConvertToLinear 0/0); tmo=1 mean 40.08 max 83; tmo=2 mean 25.73 max 88 (8-bit levels); MakeThumbnail mean 38.25 max 85; ConvertToICCProfile(sRGB) vs loaded mean 0.203 max 0.737; EXR copy vs linear truth mean 0.220 max 0.287. Conformance lossless_pfm: same pattern (Drago mean 22.9 max 84; ICC conversion max 23.8); matches its sRGB reference read as sRGB (peak 0.0014).
- Fix sketch: decode float output to linear light (XYB: JxlDecoderSetPreferredColorProfile linear; otherwise JxlDecoderSetOutputColorProfile with the default CMS), or at minimum attach libjxl's data ICC instead of returning early.
- **Fixed** in a05c232: a float page whose colours have an SDR curve (sRGB, BT.709, a gamma, DCI) and primaries with H.273 code points holds linear light, the curve undone sample by sample as FreeImage_ConvertToLinear undoes it (odd below 0, continued above 1, the alpha untouched), and is described as its result: CICP P/8, the linear profile of P3 or BT.2020 primaries, none for sRGB's. All three cases, and BT.709, gamma and P3 files, now tone map, thumbnail and convert to sRGB exactly as through ConvertToLinear; lossless sRGB, BT.709 and P3 pages equal ConvertToLinear of the file's samples bit for bit (gamma 2.2 within 3e-7, from the exponent the file stores), lossy ones are within 7e-7 of libjxl's own linear rendering. Left as they were: linear files (libjxl's ICC stays), PQ (FITM_PQ), HLG, colours libjxl cannot name, the TO_ICC route. Costs: an sRGB-curve float file saved again lossless is written as linear sRGB (same light, other samples), a float page an application tags with such a curve comes back from the multi-page cache as linear light, and loading takes ~160 ms more per 12 MP. EXR copies of P3 or BT.2020 pages still lose the primaries: the EXR writer stores none. conformance.py makes lossless_pfm's reference linear through ConvertToLinear (its reference.icc has a cicp tag): still exact.

## JXL-2 Lossless saves of float images with negative samples fail at efforts 1-3, so JXL multi-page documents refuse such pages
- Class: new-bug. Severity: Medium. Confidence: high.
- Where: MultiPage.cpp:436-438 (page-cache flags for FIF_JXL = JXL_LOSSLESS | 0x10000 = lossless effort 1); PluginJXL.cpp:1873-1877, :1960-1962 (float frames as 32-bit IEEE samples); libjxl enc_modular.cc:174-178 copies float bits into int32, fast predictor paths enc_encoding.cc:314/338/366 return "Residual overflow" when sign-bit values mix with positives; 4b3d2f4.
- What: Save/SaveToMemory with JXL_LOSSLESS | (1..3 << 16) fails ("Cannot encode the image: encoder error") for FLOAT/RGBF/RGBAF with any negative sample (even -0.001 or -0.0). Negatives are routine (ConvertToLinear(FI_LINEAR_SRGB_PRIMARIES) of wide gamut, Lanczos, cjxl PFM). The cache stores every appended/inserted/edited page with these flags: AppendPage/InsertPage return FALSE, UnlockPage(changed) drops the edit. At effort 7 real images pass; small flat ones can fail (8x8 FLOAT with one -0.001 fails at efforts 1-7, passes >= 8). Positive floats, 8/16-bit, CMYK round-trip exactly.
- Evidence (src/floatsave.cpp, effort3/4.cpp, lanczos.cpp): lossless_pfm 500x500 RGBF (241880 negatives): JXL_LOSSLESS effort 7 ok (974286 bytes); JXL_LOSSLESS|0x10000 FAILED; "FreeImage_AppendPage: the page could not be stored"; AppendPage 0, pages=0, CloseMultiBitmap 1, no file written. rgb16_pq_lossless / icc_sampled_lossy after ConvertToLinear(FI_LINEAR_SRGB_PRIMARIES): same. Lanczos-upscaled float checkerboard: e1-e3 fail, e4-e9 ok. ASan/LSan clean.
- Fix sketch: a cache format that stores floats exactly for FIF_JXL float pages, or retry failed lossless float encodes at effort >= 8 (passed in every case tested, not proven).
- **Fixed** in 24cdd28, in PluginJXL alone (libjxl is not patched, by decision): a lossless float frame with both signs in a channel, or at effort 3, is written with libjxl's select predictor at effort 4 at least (efforts 1-3 ignore the predictor), which none of libjxl's refusing paths take; every other image is written byte for byte as before. Cause: libjxl 0.12's overflow checks (upstream #4759, #4794) refuse residuals past 32 bits that its decoder, jxl-oxide and jxl-rs add back modulo 2^32 (upstream issue #4902; PR #4948, which wraps them, unmerged). Worse than reported: FLOAT -1e-6, 0.25 failed at every effort, effort >= 8 included; effort 3's weighted tree refuses one sign too (0 beside 60000 in 3 of 8 random images, 0 beside 3e38 in 8 of 8). Not seen in 11760 targeted encodes: a refusal of one sign at efforts 4-10. Costs: select within 1% of libjxl's own choice on HDR files and wide-gamut photos as linear sRGB, but on synthetic smooth data up to 50% larger (efforts 5-7) or 50% smaller (8-10) where libjxl's choice also worked; such frames at efforts 1-2 take effort 4's time, ~3.5x, the cache's included.
- **libjxl fixed too** in 212abd0: upstream PR #4948's residual fix ported (the three refusing paths and PixelSub; not its cost-estimate, palette or cleanup changes), so libjxl's own predictors no longer refuse these samples. The plugin's select predictor stays as a second guard, for a libjxl upgrade that drops the port before upstream merges #4948; it also kept frames with both signs from libjxl's undecodable effort 10 files, which JXL-7's fix (a6698e1) avoids for every lossless float frame.

## JXL-3 Animations saved through the multi-page API size the canvas from the first two pages and silently crop or drop later frames
- Class: new-bug. Severity: Medium (silent data loss; pages from FreeImage's own loaders carry LogicalWidth/Height and are safe; app-assembled mixed-size frames hit it). Confidence: high.
- Where: PluginJXL.cpp:2259-2290 (StartAnimation canvas = union of first and second + their LogicalWidth/Height; called at :2519 when page 2 arrives); AddFrame clips silently (:2122-2132: off-canvas frames become one transparent pixel, larger ones cut); 4b3d2f4. qpv's WebP/APNG/GIF writers widen the canvas to cover every frame (PluginWebP.cpp:1074).
- Evidence (src/sizes.cpp, src/canvas.c): append 24-bit pages 10x10, 12x8, 40x30 (no tags): jxl pages=3: 12x10 12x10 12x10 (page 2 was 40x30); webp/APNG/gif 10x10 12x8 40x30 (canvas 40x30); no message from the JXL plugin.
- Fix sketch: hold pages until Close and size the canvas over all (as WebP), or at least warn when clipping/dropping.
- Fixed in 918ca74 (every page held until Close, the canvas sized over all of them), **reverted** in 27d4e18 by decision: holding every page uncompressed until Close costs too much memory. Open again. Also affected: TestAPI/JXL regress's own 7-frame animation, whose last frame reaches 66x52 past the 64x48 the pages ask for, is cut, and the test's compositor cuts it alike, so it passes. libjxl takes the canvas size before the first frame, so any complete fix holds the pages in some form; cheaper routes left: a warning when a frame is cut or dropped, or pages held compressed (CPU instead of memory).

## JXL-4 Cut lossless (modular) JPEG XL files are refused although libjxl can return most of the decoded image
- Class: new-bug. Severity: Low. Confidence: high.
- Where: PluginJXL.cpp:1089-1099 (JxlDecoderFlushImage only after a JXL_DEC_FRAME_PROGRESSION event); :1115-1122 (no pixels -> NULL); 4b3d2f4. VarDCT cut files salvage fine (warning "shown at a lower resolution").
- Evidence: patches_lossless/input.jxl (34207 bytes) cut to 90%/99%: "Cannot decode the image: the file is cut short" NULL; libjxl direct (src/flushtest.cpp) at 99%: progression events 0, pixels before flush 0, after flush 100%; src/flushtest2.cpp flushed vs full: identical pixels 56.4/91.7/99.5% at cuts 60/90/99% (black 8.5/1.7/0.4%); caveat: ~35% at 60% belong to partially decoded groups.
- Fix sketch: on JXL_DEC_NEED_MORE_INPUT with output set, also try JxlDecoderFlushImage for non-progressive frames; keep the damaged-file warning.
- **Fixed** in a4e8434: a frame cut short that has shown no pixel is flushed (before, only after a progression event, which libjxl sends for VarDCT frames without extra channels alone); a flush that shows one colour everywhere, or nothing but transparency, decoded no group, and the load is refused as before (a cut image of one colour so too). A frame that showed its decoded groups keeps them and its blank rest, as before. Wider than reported: libjxl decodes as a whole, showing nothing until flushed, palette images, squeezed (lossy modular) ones and those whose alpha takes a palette, so FreeImage's own lossless RGBA saves and lossy RGBA files (VarDCT with such an alpha) were refused at every cut too. In 3042 cuts (40 per file) of the conformance inputs, TestAPI/JXL's data and 11 files FreeImage writes, 10 lossless: the 908 that loaded load byte for byte as before, with the same message; 276 more load (patches_lossless from 57.5% on), all but 4 with a whole decoded 128x128 block; those 4 are lower-resolution renderings, or, at 57.5% of patches_lossless, its first palette colour under the patches. 2591 page locks of 13 cut animations are unchanged: libjxl flushes no frame that blends or is cropped. The rest is libjxl's fill, not blank (the first palette colour, a lower-resolution approximation, patches and splines drawn over it), so the warning says what could not be decoded is filled in by the decoder. Left: a cut between the decoder's global sections and its first whole group loads as the fill alone when the fill is not one colour; cut CMYK files stay refused (LoadCMYK salvages nothing). TestAPI/JXL under ASan and the cut sweeps linked to its instrumented objects: clean.

## JXL-6 Lossless float animations saved through the multi-page API recolour every frame after the first
- Class: new-bug, found while fixing JXL-2. Severity: Medium for wide-gamut frames. Confidence: high.
- Where: PluginJXL.cpp AddFrame: ConvertToLayout turns RGBF pages into RGBAF with FreeImage_ConvertToRGBAF, which drops the ICC profile (ConvertTo* do), so ConvertToFileProfile then converts frames after the first from FreeImage's default, linear sRGB, to the file's profile; pages read back from the page cache carry libjxl's profile of their colours.
- Evidence (three identical 64x48 RGBF pages of linear light from ConvertToLinear, CloseMultiBitmap with JXL_LOSSLESS, pages read back): sRGB pages 1-2 off by up to 1.0e-4, Display P3 pages 1-2 off by up to 0.14, page 0 exact in both; the same before and after JXL-1 and JXL-2.
- Fix sketch: convert a frame to the file's profile before ConvertToLayout, or give the converted frame the page's profile.
- **Fixed** in 20a08f8: a frame's colours are converted to the file's profile while it still carries its own, then its pixel format. CMYK pages are rendered to sRGB first, as before, so every frame of a CMYK animation stays the profile's perceptual rendering; converting them straight to the file's profile would have made frames after the first relative colorimetric with black-point compensation, 5 to 20 levels from the first with FOGRA39, SWOP and Ghostscript's CMYK profiles. Wider than reported: every page the layout converts (24-bit, palette, grey, RGB16, RGBF and the rest) lost its profile, lossless and lossy: 24-bit Display P3 pages came back off by up to 26 levels, ProPhoto 63, RGB16 Adobe RGB 0.11, RGBF linear Display P3 0.12, linear sRGB 4.5e-4; a page with a CICP tag alone gets libjxl's profile of its colours in the page cache and lost it too (BT.709 curve: 16 levels), so re-saving a JPEG XL animation without alpha whose colours are not sRGB recoloured every frame but the first. Now every such page comes back as the first, bit for bit; pages already RGBA, CMYK pages and untagged 8- and 16-bit pages are written byte for byte as before (198 A/B files of stills and animations: the 17 that differ are JXL-6's and JXL-7's cases), but for untagged grey pages in an animation with a profile, converted from FreeImage's grey profile now, not as RGB: within one step. Cost: a page of fewer bits than the file's is converted at its own depth: untagged 24-bit pages in an RGBA16 Display P3 animation land up to half an 8-bit level further from the exact rendering (0.0058 at most, was 0.0039), while 24-bit Display P3 pages in a linear float animation, taken for linear sRGB before, are right (0.0022 at most, was 0.30). Left: 8-bit pages in a 16-bit animation keep FreeImage_ConvertToRGBA16's 8-bit shift (alpha 65280, so such a frame lets 0.4% of the canvas through), and 8-bit pages without a profile in a float animation stay on their curve, written as linear light. An allocation-failure sweep over the close of an edited animation whose pages take every one of these conversions (24-bit Display P3 and Adobe RGB, RGB16 Adobe RGB, CMYK with a profile; each of the close's 29841 allocations failed once) leaves the original file or a complete one; with LeakSanitizer (every 7th) nothing leaks in AddFrame or the conversions; the reader's leaks it found are JXL-8.

## JXL-7 Lossless float saves at effort 10 can write files libjxl cannot decode
- Class: new-bug in the vendored libjxl 0.12.0 encoder, found while porting upstream PR #4948; libjxl not fixed, avoided in PluginJXL. Severity: High (silent data loss: the save returns TRUE). Confidence: high.
- Where: at effort 10 libjxl codes lossless modular images with its Variable predictor; some float images coded so fail to decode ("the data is invalid or not supported"). Every single predictor, Best included, and Variable at effort 9 decode. Unrelated to the sample signs and to PR #4948: the same without it.
- Evidence (256x192 crops as linear sRGB floats): FreeImage_Save with JXL_LOSSLESS | (10 << 16) of all-positive RGBF crops of the conformance bike (448,1776) and bicycles (576,439) images returns TRUE, then FreeImage_Load gives NULL; the same crops as RGB16 or 24-bit load bit exact. Through libjxl: 8 of 20 crops of photos converted to float undecodable at effort 10 (cafe 5 of 5, bike 2, bicycles 1, progressive 0, and 1 more with its negatives set to 0), none of 15 crops of the HDR files leadenhall, rogland and orange_colorballs. 24cdd28's select predictor happens to protect frames with both signs in a channel.
- Fix sketch: in PluginJXL, give lossless float frames at effort 10 a predictor other than Variable (Best decodes every case tested), or cap them at effort 9; report upstream.
- **Fixed** in a6698e1, in PluginJXL alone (libjxl is not changed and nothing is reported upstream, by decision): a lossless float frame, still or in an animation, asked for effort 10 is written at effort 9. At effort 10 libjxl encodes a lossless image 22 to 26 ways and keeps the smallest, for float images at times one its decoder refuses. Lossy frames skip those trials, which libjxl runs for lossless frames alone (the failing crops saved lossy at effort 10 load, as floats too), and no 8- or 16-bit save failed (168 lossless saves at effort 10 of crops of 7 conformance images, six layouts), so those keep effort 10; every other file is written byte for byte as before. A crop of TestAPI/JXL's test-jxl.jxl (13th frame as played, 457,631, 256x192, as linear sRGB) is undecodable at effort 10 and now loads back bit exact in regress. Cost: such files are effort 9's, 8 and 19% larger than effort 10 made them where it worked (1024x768 crops of cafe and bike), 2 to 11% at 256x192, written 10 to 16 times faster.

## JXL-8 A failed allocation while a JPEG XL file is read leaks libjxl's decoder
- Class: new-bug, found while checking JXL-6's fix, not fixed. Severity: Low (only after an allocation fails). Confidence: high.
- Where: PluginJXL.cpp ParseHeaders: a std::bad_alloc thrown inside JxlDecoderProcessInput, or by the reader's own vectors, leaves the function without JxlDecoderDestroy(dec), and Open's catch cannot reach the local decoder. Vendored libjxl: JxlDecoderCreate and JxlEncoderCreate lose their block when its construction throws (decode.cc:850, encode.cc:2035), and an interrupted JxlDecoderProcessInput loses the objects it was building (under LoadPixels and LoadCMYK); FreeImage's catches cannot reach those.
- Evidence (sweep67-lsan.cpp in .claude/scratch/jxl-6-7: the close of an edited 4-page animation, every 7th of its 29841 allocations failed with operator new throwing, LeakSanitizer in each child): 356 of 4266 failure points leak, the same at the points compared on 3d80f66 (both reading one original file); of 2322 records, most are ParseHeaders' decoder with what it held, then objects of libjxl's ProcessSections under LoadPixels and LoadCMYK, PrepareDecoder's JxlDecoderCreate, and StartEncoder's JxlEncoderCreate (2832 bytes). Every run still ends with a complete file or the original kept.
- Fix sketch: destroy the decoder on every exit of ParseHeaders (catch, destroy, rethrow); the libjxl losses need libjxl changes.
